Practice room

Learn to beatmatch without touching your gear

Beatmatching is the one DJ skill you cannot read your way into. So we built the drill: two synthesised loops, a pitch fader, a jog nudge, and a score that measures how long you kept the kicks locked. It runs in the browser, takes 45 seconds a go, and needs no account, download or controller.

  • Tempo first, then phase. The readout shows which one you are actually getting wrong.
  • Turn the numbers off. A training-wheels-free mode leaves you the two decks and your ears.
  • Then take it to the guide. The drill pairs with our written beatmatching walkthrough and four-week plan.
