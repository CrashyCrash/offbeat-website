Affiliate disclosure & commercial policy

How Offbeat earns money, how affiliate links work, which programs we participate in, and why commissions never dictate an editorial recommendation.

Offbeat Editorial TeamUpdated October 20264 min read
Browse all guidesHow we research

Effective date: October 2026 · Reviewed & current

The short version: We earn a referral commission when you purchase gear or software through certain links on Offbeat, at zero extra cost to you. We never accept paid product placements, we never sell rankings, and commission rates have zero influence on what we recommend.

How affiliate links work on Offbeat

Offbeat is an independently owned publication. Producing in-depth guides, building interactive tools like our Beatmatch Trainer and DJ Rig Planner, and tracking hardware firmware updates requires sustained time and effort. Rather than putting our content behind a paid paywall or cluttering pages with flashing display ads, we fund our work through affiliate partnerships.

When you click an outbound link to an online retailer (such as Amazon or authorized audio dealers) or a software developer and complete a qualifying purchase, that merchant pays us a small percentage of the sale as a referral fee. This does not increase the price you pay by a single cent. In many cases, it connects you to current verified retailer stock and seasonal discounts.

Programs we participate in

Our affiliate relationships primarily include:

  • Amazon Associates: As an Amazon Associate, Offbeat Inc. earns from qualifying purchases made through our tagged links (Associate tag: offbeatdj-20). We link to Amazon searches and product pages for DJ controllers, headphones, studio monitors, cables, and accessories.
  • Software & Digital Music Services: When you purchase or subscribe to DJ software, DAWs, plugin suites, or digital distribution platforms through verified partner links, we may receive an affiliate referral credit.
  • Authorized Music Retailers: We partner with established audio retailers to provide direct links to in-stock equipment and warranty-backed products.

Why commission never alters our editorial verdicts

The single most important principle of our publishing model is editorial independence. If readers cannot trust our recommendations, our publication has no value. Here is how we ensure commercial links never distort our advice:

Zero paid rankingsNo brand or manufacturer can pay to be ranked #1, added to a shortlist, or featured in an editorial roundup. Rankings are determined entirely by our editorial research.
Recommending used and cheaper gearWe regularly recommend second-hand hardware (like used Traktor Kontrol S2 units) or lower-cost equipment over expensive flagship models when it is the smarter choice for a beginner — even though cheaper items generate far smaller commissions.
Highlighting flaws and limitationsEvery product guide on Offbeat includes verified limitations and buying caveats (like plasticky chassis, missing balanced outputs, or required subscription tiers). We will never soften a criticism to protect an affiliate sale.
Free alternatives are always includedWhere free software or drills exist (such as Serato DJ Lite, VirtualDJ Home, free rekordbox, or our browser tools), we highlight them prominently so readers aren't pressured into unnecessary spending.

How affiliate links are labelled across the site

We believe transparency should be immediate and unmissable. You will notice our commercial disclosures in several clear places:

  • Top of Page Disclosure: Every article containing affiliate links features a visible notice directly below the hero header stating: "Disclosure: This page uses affiliate links. We may earn a commission at no extra cost to you."
  • Button & Link Formatting: Outbound commercial buttons are clearly labelled with retailer destinations (e.g. "Search Pioneer DDJ-FLX4 on Amazon →") and carry appropriate rel="nofollow noopener sponsored" attributes in compliance with search engine guidelines.
  • Footer Notice: Every page on the site carries a persistent footer statement linking back to this full disclosure policy.

FTC compliance & legal transparency

This disclosure is published in strict compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines concerning the use of endorsements and testimonials in advertising (16 C.F.R. Part 255). It ensures that all material commercial connections between Offbeat Inc. and retail merchants or product manufacturers are clearly disclosed to our readers.

If you have any questions regarding our commercial practices, affiliate relationships, or specific product links, please contact our team directly at hello@offbeatinc.com or visit our contact page.

Our commitment to readers

We are DJs and music makers first. We write the guides we wish existed when we started playing out — honest, specific, and free of marketing fluff. Thank you for supporting Offbeat when you purchase through our links.
