What Offbeat is for

Most DJ buying advice online is either a spec sheet with an affiliate link stapled to it, or a forum thread that assumes you already know what a DVS licence is. Offbeat exists in the space between: plain explanations of how the gear and software actually fit together, written so that someone buying their first controller and someone upgrading to a four-channel rig can both use the same page.

We publish three kinds of work — buying guides that compare current models within a budget, reviews that look at one product in depth, and skills guides that explain how to use the equipment once you own it. Category hubs connect them so you can move from "what should I buy?" to "how do I use it?" without going back to a search engine.

How a guide gets made

1

Start with the primary sources

Manufacturer specification sheets, software support and compatibility pages, licence and streaming documentation, and current retailer listings. Prices are recorded with the month they were checked.

2

Write for a decision, not a keyword

Every buying page has to answer three things: who this is for, what it replaces, and what you give up at the price. If a page cannot answer those, it does not ship.

3

Say what we did and did not do

Where a judgement rests on hands-on use, the page says so. Where it rests on documentation and retailer data, it says that instead. We do not invent lab measurements, test rigs or numeric scores to sound authoritative.

4

Review and correct

Editors check specifications against the cited sources before publication. After that, reader corrections are the main input: when a price, spec or compatibility note changes, the page is updated rather than rewritten around it.

Automation helps with the mechanical parts — checking that prices are dated, that tables and links are intact, that a page does not contradict itself. It does not choose the recommendations.

What we will not do

No paid placementBrands cannot buy a position in a guide, a score, or a mention. Commission rates never determine ranking — several of our most recommended products earn us nothing.
No invented testingWe do not claim to have measured latency, weighed gear on a bench or run controlled listening tests we did not run.
No stale prices presented as currentPrice notes carry the month and the kind of source they came from, and every buying page tells you what to confirm before paying.
No filler pagesWe would rather publish 115 useful guides than 500 thin ones, and we retire pages that no longer help anyone.

How Offbeat is funded

Offbeat is reader-supported through affiliate links. If you buy through a retailer link on this site, we may receive a commission at no extra cost to you. That is the entire business model: no sponsored posts, no brand-owned rankings, no display-ad networks following you around the site.
