Every guide and interactive tool on Offbeat is produced by working DJs and music writers. When you send an email to hello@offbeatinc.com, it lands directly in front of the editors who research and maintain the site.

How to get the fastest response

To help us address your email efficiently, here is what to include based on your reason for reaching out:

Factual correctionsIf you spot an outdated price, a changed software specification, or a factual error, send us the exact guide URL, the specific line that needs updating, and an official manufacturer or retailer source link. Verified corrections are updated within 48 hours.
DJ gear & setup adviceTell us your target budget, the equipment you already own (laptop, headphones, speakers), and where you plan to play (bedroom practice, house parties, club booths, mobile weddings). We read every query, and frequent reader questions inspire our new deep-dive guides.
Accessibility & site bugsIf you encounter a barrier using a screen reader, keyboard navigation, or mobile browser, tell us your device, browser, and what occurred. We treat accessibility reports as urgent software bugs.
Review samples & pressManufacturers and audio brands are welcome to send hardware for evaluation. Note our policy: we do not accept payment for positive coverage, review units do not come with pre-publication copy approval, and we publish verified drawbacks openly.

What we do not respond to

To protect our time and maintain editorial integrity, our inbox automatically filters and discards:

  • Unsolicited guest post pitches: All Offbeat guides are written by our in-house editorial team. We do not publish third-party sponsored content.
  • Paid link exchange requests: We never sell backlinks, participate in PBN link networks, or swap links for payment.
  • "Pay to rank #1" placement offers: Rankings in our buying guides and comparisons cannot be bought or influenced by commercial commissions.
